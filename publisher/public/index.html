What we access, and why.

Only the permissions posting needs, each used for what it says. Tokens are encrypted, and you can disconnect an account or delete everything at any time.

TikTok

user.info.basic
Your nickname and avatar, to show which account you post to.
video.publish
Publishing the photo posts you confirm, with the privacy, comment and disclosure settings you chose.
video.upload
Part of TikTok's Content Posting API. We never send drafts to your inbox.

Instagram

instagram_business_basic
Your username and picture, and the like and comment counts of the posts we published.
instagram_business_content_publish
Publishing the photos and carousels you schedule.

Facebook Pages

pages_show_list

Questions.

Do I need a password?

No. You sign in with a code we email you. Each social account connects on that platform's own screen, so we never see your TikTok, Instagram or Facebook password.

Which Instagram accounts work?

Professional accounts (Business or Creator). A personal account can switch to professional for free in Instagram's settings.

Does it work when my laptop is closed?

Yes. Posts go out from our servers at the time you set, in your workspace's time zone.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Invite people as admins or members, and limit members to the brands they work on. Each workspace is fully separate.

Which TikTok privacy options can I choose?

Exactly the ones your TikTok account offers, read from TikTok each time. The choice starts empty so it is always yours. Branded content can't be posted as "Only me".

Can I change a scheduled post?

Yes, until it starts publishing: fix the caption, move it to another day, or cancel it from the calendar.

Does it post videos?

Photo posts and carousels today. Video, Reels and YouTube Shorts are next.

Who makes this?

The team behind ARCO: Day Planner & Focus. ARCO's own accounts post with it every day.
