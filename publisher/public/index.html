How it works

Four steps, and the account owner is in control at each one.

1Connect Sign in with TikTok. We read the account's nickname and avatar and show them at the top of the form, so it is always clear which account will post.
2Choose a post Pick one of the prepared ARCO slideshows. The slides are hosted on ARCO's site and shown as a preview before anything is sent.
3Review the settings Who can view the post is read from your TikTok settings and has no default. Comments, music and commercial disclosure are explicit choices.
4Post The post goes to TikTok through the Content Posting API. The page shows TikTok's processing status until the post is live on the profile.

What we access

Only three permissions, and nothing is stored on our side.

PermissionUsed forDetails
user.info.basicShow who you are posting as Nickname, username and avatar, displayed on the form. Never stored.
video.publishPublish the slideshow you approved Direct post with the privacy level, interaction and disclosure settings you chose.
video.uploadSend a draft to your TikTok inbox instead Used when a post should be finished inside the TikTok app rather than published directly.

Your session is a signed cookie in your own browser that expires with the TikTok access token. There is no database, no analytics and no tracking. Disconnect removes the cookie immediately, and you can revoke access at any time from TikTok's Settings › Security › Apps and services.

FAQ

Do I need an ARCO account or password?

No. The only sign-in is TikTok's own. If you can log in to TikTok, you can use the publisher.

Which privacy levels can I choose?

Exactly the ones your TikTok account offers. We ask TikTok for them every time the page loads, and the dropdown starts empty so the choice is always yours.

What do "Promotional content" and "Paid partnership" mean?

They are TikTok's disclosure labels. Turning on Disclose post content and picking Your Brand labels the post as promotional content; picking Branded Content labels it as a paid partnership. Branded content cannot be posted as "Only me".

Why are Duet and Stitch off?

They only apply to video posts. This tool publishes photo slideshows, so those controls are shown but cannot be turned on.

Can I edit or delete a post afterwards?

Once published, the post belongs to your TikTok account. Edit or delete it in the TikTok app as with any other post.

Who is this for?

The ARCO team, publishing to ARCO's TikTok channels. Every post goes through the same review step on this page before it reaches TikTok.
