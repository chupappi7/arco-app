Something did not post

The status box under the Post button shows TikTok's own processing result. The most common causes are: the TikTok token expired (press Disconnect, then sign in again), the account has reached TikTok's limit of five pending posts in 24 hours, or a transient internal_error from TikTok, which clears on the next attempt.

The privacy dropdown is missing an option

We show exactly the privacy levels your TikTok account allows, read fresh on every page load. A private account, for example, cannot post to "Everyone". "Only me" disappears when Branded Content is selected, because TikTok does not allow branded content to be private.

Disconnecting and revoking access

Disconnect on the posting page deletes the session cookie in your browser. To revoke the app entirely, open TikTok, go to Settings and privacy › Security & permissions › Apps and services, and remove ARCO Publisher.
