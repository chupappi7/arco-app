How it works

Publisher is organised the way you already think about it. A brand owns its TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts, its posting times and its voice. Its library folders hold posts that are ready to go: a folder is one post. The AI works in those terms, so you never have to mention account IDs or file names.

1 · YouDrop content into library folders, with a note if it needs one.
2 · You ask"Schedule Bakery's new posts for next week."
3 · AI plansWrites captions in the brand's voice, picks free slots and checks every platform rule.
4 · You checkIt shows you the plan. Change anything, or say "go".
5 · ScheduledThe posts appear in your calendar, marked as made by AI, and can be undone as one batch.

Set up Publisher

  1. Group accounts into brands. Go to Accounts, make a brand (for example "Bakery") and add its TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts. If you only have one brand, the AI simply uses all your accounts.
  2. Set posting times. On each brand, set the days and times you like to post, such as Mon to Fri at 12:30 and 18:00. When you don't name a time, the AI fills these slots. Also check the workspace time zone in Settings → Workspace.
  3. Write a brand voice (optional, but it pays off). A few lines on how the brand sounds, plus two or three example captions. The AI reads it before writing anything.
  4. Create an API key. In Settings → Developer, the workspace owner presses Create API key. Copy it somewhere safe: it is shown only once, and it starts with apk_.

Organise the library

One folder is one post. Each folder holds a post's photos in order (the first one is the cover), or one video. It belongs to a brand and can carry a note.

Bakery ├─ Black Friday promo 6 photos · note: "20% off, code BF20, not before Friday" ● unused ├─ Croissant behind the scenes 8 photos ● unused └─ Halloween cookies 5 photos ✓ posted Oct 30 ARCO └─ Focus mode explained 1 video · note: "link in bio" ◷ scheduled Nov 18
  • The note is your brief: the offer, a discount code, what to stress, what to avoid, "not before Friday". The AI writes the caption from the photos, the note and the brand's voice.
  • The status keeps itself up to date: unused, awaiting approval, scheduled or posted. That's how "schedule the new ones" works, and why nothing gets posted twice by accident.
  • Filling folders: upload in Publisher's Library, or ask the AI to do it. "Make a Bakery folder called Black Friday from these photos" works with files the assistant can reach, or with image links.
  • Photos are JPG or PNG up to 5 MB. Instagram only takes JPG and at most 10 photos. Videos are MP4 or MOV up to 100 MB, one per folder.

Connect your AI

Publisher's MCP server address:

https://arco-publisher.vercel.app/mcp

It signs in with your API key, sent as Authorization: Bearer apk_…. Choose your app:

Run this once in a terminal, with your key in place of apk_…:

claude mcp add --transport http arco-publisher https://arco-publisher.vercel.app/mcp \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer apk_…"

Then start claude and ask away. Type /mcp to check it shows as connected. Claude Code can also read photos from a folder on your computer and upload them for you.

Open Settings → Developer → Edit Config and add this to claude_desktop_config.json (you need Node.js installed):

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "arco-publisher": {
      "command": "npx",
      "args": ["-y", "mcp-remote", "https://arco-publisher.vercel.app/mcp",
               "--header", "Authorization:${ARCO_AUTH}"],
      "env": { "ARCO_AUTH": "Bearer apk_…" }
    }
  }
}

Restart Claude Desktop. ARCO Publisher then shows up in the tools menu of a new chat.

Any app that supports remote MCP servers over HTTP with headers, such as Cursor, Windsurf or VS Code, takes roughly this:

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "arco-publisher": {
      "url": "https://arco-publisher.vercel.app/mcp",
      "headers": { "Authorization": "Bearer apk_…" }
    }
  }
}

Custom connectors on claude.ai sign in with OAuth, which Publisher doesn't offer yet. For now, use Claude Desktop or Claude Code. They use the same account and the same chats work.

Treat the key like a password.It can do anything a workspace admin can: schedule, cancel and read stats for every brand. Don't paste it into shared documents or chats. If it leaks, create a new one in Settings → Developer, which switches the old one off immediately.

Approval and undo

Approval is on by default. Posts the AI schedules wait in Drafts, marked as AI posts, at their planned time. They go out only after someone approves them in Publisher. For TikTok, you also choose who can see the post when you approve it. Once you trust the results, the workspace owner can switch approval off. The AI then schedules directly, but it still has to choose TikTok's privacy for each post.

Whichever mode you use:

  • Preview first. The AI checks the whole plan and shows it to you before anything is saved: each post's time, accounts and caption, plus any problems such as an Instagram post with too many photos.
  • One batch per run. Everything made in one go gets a batch number, and "undo that" cancels whatever from it hasn't been published yet.
  • It's all visible. AI posts appear in the calendar like any other post, and you can edit or cancel them there.

What the AI can do

ToolWhat it's for
get_overviewBrands, their accounts, posting times, voice, library counts, free slots, time zone and approval mode. The AI starts here.
list_foldersLibrary folders with photos, notes and status, optionally narrowed to one brand or status.
create_folder, update_folder, delete_folderMake, rename, re-note, move or reorder folders.
upload_to_folderAdd photos (as a link or the file itself) or a video (as a link) to a folder.
find_slotsA brand's next free posting times.
schedule_postsPlan up to 30 posts and check them; then, once you agree, schedule them.
list_postsWhat's scheduled, waiting for approval, published or failed, in any time window.
update_post, cancel_post, retry_postChange a post's caption or time, cancel it, or retry a failed one.
undo_batchCancel everything from one scheduling run that hasn't been published yet.
get_statsViews, likes, comments, shares and followers against the previous period, plus the top posts.

What it can't do: connect or disconnect accounts, invite or remove people, change the API key, or delete brands. Those stay in Publisher.

For developers: the API

The same features are plain HTTPS under https://arco-publisher.vercel.app/api/v1, with the header Authorization: Key apk_…. Times are epoch seconds, or 2026-11-20T18:00 in the workspace's time zone.

CallDoes
GET /folders?brand=&status=List folders. status: unused, awaiting_approval, scheduled, posted.
POST /folders{name, brand_id, note}
PATCH /folders/:id{name, note, brand_id, order: [keys]}
DELETE /folders/:id?remove_items=1Delete the folder. Its items stay in the library unless remove_items is set.
POST /media then POST /libraryUpload a photo (raw body, Content-Type: image/jpeg), then file it with {keys, folder_id}.
GET /slots?brand=&count=&from=Free posting slots.
POST /plans{posts: [...], confirm}. Same input as the schedule_posts tool. Without confirm: true it only previews.
POST /batches/:id/undoCancel a run's unpublished posts.
POST /posts/:id/approveApprove a draft: {privacy_level, at}, both optional.
GET /agenda?from=&to=&brand=&status=Posts in a window, drafts included.
GET /stats?days=&platform=&brand=Results.
PATCH /workspace{ai_review: true|false} switches approval for AI posts on or off.

A post in a plan looks like this:

{
  "folder_id": "fld_…",               // or "library_keys": ["…", "…"]
  "brand": "Bakery",                  // default: the folder's brand
  "platforms": ["tiktok", "instagram"],  // optional
  "at": "2026-11-20T18:00",           // leave out for the next free slot
  "caption": "Fresh at 4am 🥐",
  "captions": { "instagram": "Fresh at 4am 🥐 #bakery #croissant" },
  "tiktok": { "privacy_level": "PUBLIC_TO_EVERYONE" }
}

Limits and safety

  • Timing: posts must be at least 5 minutes ahead. A workspace can have up to 200 posts waiting and 200 drafts.
  • Plans: up to 30 posts each, and up to 10 files per upload call.
  • Library: up to 300 folders and 500 items, and 1 GB of storage per workspace.
  • Platform rules are checked on every post, exactly as in the app: Instagram's 10 photos, JPG only, 2,200 characters and 30 hashtags; TikTok's privacy options for each account; Reels length; and so on.
  • Scope: the AI only sees and touches the workspace its key belongs to.

Troubleshooting

You seeDo this
"Sign in first" / 401The key is missing, mistyped or replaced. Make sure the header reads Bearer apk_…, with no extra spaces or quotes.
"No free posting slot"The brand has no posting times, or they're all taken for 4 weeks. Set times on the brand, or give the post a time.
"Instagram only accepts JPG photos"Convert the photos to JPG, or post that folder to TikTok and Facebook only.
"Choose who can view this post"Approval is off, so TikTok needs a privacy choice for each post. Tell the AI, for example "public on TikTok".
A post failedAsk "why did it fail?" (the reason is on the post), fix the cause, then "retry it". Accounts that need reconnecting are skipped and named in the plan.
Times are an hour offSet the workspace time zone in Settings → Workspace. Without it, the AI works in UTC.
