# ARCO Publisher redesign

Goal: make ARCO Publisher look and feel like a real product for creators and
marketing managers: a side menu, an overview dashboard, a composer that shows
the post before it goes out. Simple to use, calm, and good-looking.

## What the product is about

A scheduler's subject is **time and channels**: what goes out, where, and when.
Every screen answers one of three questions:

1. What is going out next, and is anything broken? (Overview)
2. What am I posting, and how will it look on each platform? (Create)
3. When is everything going out? (Calendar)

The design leans on that: time is the main visual thread, and platform colors
(TikTok black, Instagram gradient, Facebook blue) carry the color, so the
interface around them stays quiet.

## Visual direction

**Palette: graphite and one "now" color**

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Graphite | `#1C2230` | text, primary buttons, the sidebar's active item |
| Canvas | `#F2F4F6` | app background |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | panels |
| Line | `#DFE3E8` | borders, dividers |
| Mute | `#667085` | secondary text |
| Saffron | `#E9A23B` | "now" only: the current-time marker, today, the next post going out |
| Danger | `#C2410C` | failed posts, reconnect |
| Success | `#15803D` | published |

The interface is mostly graphite on white. Saffron appears in very few places,
always meaning "now / next", so the eye finds what is about to happen. Dark mode
uses the same roles with dark surfaces.

**Type**
- Headings: **Bricolage Grotesque**, for screen titles and the overview greeting.
  It has character without being loud.
- Everything else: **Public Sans**, readable at small sizes, with tabular
  figures for times.
- Scale: 13 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 28 / 40. Sentence case everywhere: no all-caps
  labels, no eyebrow text above headings.

**Signature element: the time rail**
On the overview, today's posts sit on a vertical time rail with a saffron "now"
line that moves through the day. Posts above the line are done (published, or
failed in red); below it they are coming up. This is the one memorable thing.
Everything else stays disciplined.

**Shapes**
- Panels get a 12px radius, controls 8px, avatars and chips are round.
- Shadows only on things that float (the drawer, menus, toasts), never on every
  card.
- Plain 1px borders do the grouping.

- **Sidebar:** the workspace switcher at the top, then menu items with counts
  (drafts, failures), with Settings and the signed-in person at the bottom. It
  collapses to icons on medium screens.
- **Create** is always one click away: in the sidebar, as the top-right button,
  and with the keyboard shortcut `C`.
- A **detail drawer** slides in from the right for a post, an account or a
  person, replacing the current modal and inline editors.
- **Toasts** replace the green and red banners at the top of the page.

### Calendar

- Week and month views, restyled. Posts are thumbnails with a platform dot, and
  the saffron line marks now on today.
- Clicking a post opens the detail drawer: preview, status, fix caption, move
  time, retry or cancel, view live post.
- Filters sit in one row: brand, platform, account.

### Drafts
A grid of draft cards with a cover, the accounts and the caption start.
Clicking one opens it in Create.


- Upload by dragging files anywhere on the page, or with the button. Several at
  once, each with its own progress.
- Folders (optional), a search by file name, filters by brand and type, and a
  "Not used yet" view.
- Click a file to open the drawer: a large preview, its name, size and
  dimensions, where it has been posted, and Rename, Move, Download, Delete.
- Select several, then "Use in new post" opens Create with them as the photos.
  Create also gets "Add from library" next to uploading.
- Each workspace has a storage limit (for example 2 GB on the free plan). Files
  in the library stay until someone deletes them. Images uploaded straight into
  a post keep today's rule (deleted 7 days after the post is done), unless
  "Save to library" is ticked.

### Analytics (after the redesign)
Performance numbers pulled from the platforms (see the roadmap for what each
one gives and which permission it needs):
- A stats line on every published post: views, likes, comments, shares.
- An Analytics screen: the best posts this month, per-account follower and view
  trends, and when the audience responds best.
- The overview gets a small "Last 7 days" summary once there is data.

### Accounts
- Brand sections with account rows, the platform badge on each avatar, and the
  posting times shown inline.
- "Connect account" opens one picker: TikTok, Instagram or Facebook.
- Health is shown plainly: connected, or reconnect needed and why.

### Settings
Tabs inside the page: Workspace (name, time zone, posting times), People,
API key, Your account (sign out everywhere, workspaces).

### Sign-in and landing
- **Sign-in:** a split screen. The email-code form on one side, a calm preview
  of the product (the time rail) on the other.
- **Landing (index.html):** one screen that explains it: what it does, the
  three platforms, "Start free", then the screens. It's also what Meta's and
  TikTok's reviewers see.

## Copy

- Plain verbs: "Create post", "Post now", "Schedule", "Connect account",
  "Retry".
- An action keeps its name: the "Schedule" button leads to "Scheduled for Tue
  9:00".
- Empty states say what to do: "No posts today. Create one or fill your posting
  times."

## How it's built

- **No framework, no build step,** as today, so deploys stay one command. The
  1,500-line `app.js` gets split into ES modules: `shell.js`, `views/*.js`,
  `components.js`, `time.js`, `api.js`.
- **`tokens.css`** holds every color, radius and space as a variable, with a
  dark mode that follows the system. `app.css` holds the components.
- **One inline SVG icon set**, the same stroke weight everywhere.
- **No API changes are needed for the redesign itself.** The overview is
  computed from `/me`, `/posts` and `/drafts`. The Library and Analytics are
  the two parts that add server work.

## Phases

1. **Foundations:** tokens, fonts, icons, the app shell (sidebar, top bar,
   mobile bottom bar), and the components (buttons, inputs, chips, avatars,
   drawer, toast, tabs, empty state). Split `app.js` into modules with no
   behaviour change.
2. **Overview:** the time rail, needs attention, this week, recently published,
   and the first-run checklist.
3. **Create:** the two-pane layout, platform previews, caption tabs, the sticky
   action bar, and the folded TikTok section.
4. **Calendar and drawer:** restyled week and month views, and the post drawer.
5. **Accounts, Drafts, Settings:** restyled; Settings tabs; the connect-account
   picker.
6. **Library:** storage that keeps files, folders, upload with progress, the
   file drawer, "Use in new post" and "Add from library" in Create. This needs
   API work: a `library_items` table, folders, per-workspace storage limits, and
   posts that copy from the library.
7. **Sign-in and landing page.**
8. **Polish:** loading skeletons, keyboard shortcuts, dark mode, mobile pass,
   accessibility pass (focus, contrast, reduced motion), screenshots at
   375 / 768 / 1280.

Each phase ships on its own: deploy, check in the browser, move on.
